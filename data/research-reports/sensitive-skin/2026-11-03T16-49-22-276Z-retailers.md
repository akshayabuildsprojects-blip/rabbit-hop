## 3. Retailer-specific evidence on how shoppers discover, evaluate and buy

- Discovery often begins with creator content on TikTok or Instagram, then shoppers head to Google or YouTube to learn about ingredients and compare claims, then to retailer PDPs on Sephora or Ulta to read reviews and check price. **[Retailer-specific]** ([Beauty and Skincare Marketing Trends 2027](https://www.bigeyeagency.com/insights/beauty-and-skincare-marketing-trends-2027))
- 92% of shoppers use ratings and reviews on product pages, making PDP reviews a central evaluation checkpoint at both retailers. **[General beauty retail]** ([Beauty and Skincare Marketing Trends 2027](https://www.bigeyeagency.com/insights/beauty-and-skincare-marketing-trends-2027))
- Social creates early demand while retail media and PDP proof points such as clinical data, ingredient clarity, reviews and dermatologist support shape conversion more than polished brand claims. **[General beauty retail]** ([Beauty and Skincare Marketing Trends 2027](https://www.bigeyeagency.com/insights/beauty-and-skincare-marketing-trends-2027))
- US consumers increasingly prefer dermatologically and scientifically backed formulations over brand authority, trusting dermatologists advice at 57%, consumer reviews at 46% and scientific literature at 42%, versus 26% strongly influenced by brand image. **[General beauty retail]** ([US beauty consumers prioritize ingredient transparency over brand image](https://www.personalcareinsights.com/news/us-beauty-ingredient-trust.html))
- Only 15% fully trust clean beauty claims, so Clean at Sephora and Conscious Beauty badges function as starting filters requiring further ingredient verification. **[General beauty retail]** ([US beauty consumers prioritize ingredient transparency over brand image](https://www.personalcareinsights.com/news/us-beauty-ingredient-trust.html))
- Adverse experiences drive defection: 67% would stop using a favorite if it contained a potentially harmful ingredient, 50% would discontinue and seek safer alternative, and 46% had stopped buying certain brands because of ingredients. **[General beauty retail]** ([US beauty consumers prioritize ingredient transparency over brand image](https://www.personalcareinsights.com/news/us-beauty-ingredient-trust.html))
- Physical trial remains sensory validation, but 92% research online while in brick-and-mortar store, scanning reviews and comparing ingredients on phones. **[General beauty retail]** ([Beauty and Skincare Marketing Trends 2027](https://www.bigeyeagency.com/insights/beauty-and-skincare-marketing-trends-2027))
