## 1. Sephora: site features and shopping tools
- **[Retailer-specific]** Beauty Insider is a free rewards program where members earn 1 point per dollar on Sephora merchandise and reach VIB at $350 and Rouge at $1,000 in calendar-year spend ([About Beauty Insider Loyalty Program | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/loyalty-program)).
- **[Retailer-specific]** Members can redeem 500 points for $10 Beauty Insider Cash and redeem points for Rewards in the Rewards Bazaar ranging from 50 to 100,000 points, including deluxe samples, bundles and experiences ([About Beauty Insider Loyalty Program | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/loyalty-program)).
- **[Retailer-specific]** Sephora maintains a Trending Products page described as latest products taking social media by storm, with Only at Sephora, New and Limited Edition flags on listings ([Trending Products | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/buy/trending-products)).
- **[Retailer-specific]** Product pages have Ratings & Reviews with a five-star scale, Write a Review flow, option to add photo or video to show product in action, swatches or artistry, plus Questions & Answers and a Verified Purchase badge for eligible purchases submitted within 90 days ([Customer Service - Ratings & Reviews | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/ca/en/beauty/ratings-reviews?icid2=customer_service_ratings_reviews-write-review)).
- **[Retailer-specific]** The Beauty Insider Community has Gallery for beauty photos and videos, Ratings and Reviews, and Questions and Answers, with Gallery posts tagged to products appearing on product pages under See it in Real Life ([Beauty Insider Community FAQs](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/community-faq?icid2=customer_service_beauty_insider_community_faqs_action_link)).
- **[Retailer-specific]** Sephora Virtual Artist uses facial recognition to scan eyes, lips and cheeks for virtual try-on of eyeshadows, lip colours and false lashes, plus full looks, tutorials, outfit colour-match and side-by-side swatch comparison ([Sephora Virtual Artist: Try On Makeup Instantly | Sephora Singapore](https://admin.sephora.sg/pages/virtual-artist-sg)).
- **[Retailer-specific]** Fulfilment options include Buy Online Pick Up In Store at all locations, Pick Up Curbside at most locations, Fast Delivery from store to door, Same-Day Unlimited subscription and free standard shipping for Insiders ([In-Store Pick Up & Delivery Options | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/pick-up-delivery-options?icid2=ease_and_convenience_landing_page_link_internal)).

## 3. Retailer-specific evidence on how shoppers discover, evaluate and buy
- **[Retailer-specific]** After finding a product on TikTok, 72% of beauty consumers search for it on Google next while only 7% buy immediately in the app, indicating social discovery followed by external verification ([Discovered on Social, Decided Everywhere](https://www.stellarising.com/blog/beauty-social-search-discovered-on-social)).
- **[Retailer-specific]** After TikTok discovery, 48% buy on Amazon, 10% at Sephora, 9% on TikTok Shop and 8% on brand sites, showing comparison across touchpoints and a separate fulfilment touchpoint rather than immediate social checkout ([Discovered on Social, Decided Everywhere](https://www.stellarising.com/blog/beauty-social-search-discovered-on-social)).
- **[Retailer-specific]** Sephora launched on TikTok Shop with a livestreamed shopping session hosted by Mikayla Nogueira, initially limited to POV Beauty and Sephora Collection products on a planned monthly drop model ([Why Sephora released a beauty set exclusively on TikTok Shop](https://digiday.com/marketing/why-sephora-released-a-beauty-set-exclusively-on-tiktok-shop/)).
- **[Retailer-specific]** That launch positioned a 12-piece POV Beauty set as directing shoppers from Sephora's site to TikTok Shop, while TikTok Shop lets customers add to cart without leaving the page ([Why Sephora released a beauty set exclusively on TikTok Shop](https://digiday.com/marketing/why-sephora-released-a-beauty-set-exclusively-on-tiktok-shop/)).
- **[Retailer-specific]** Ulta Beauty joined TikTok Shop in July with a perfume launch, in a context where TikTok Shop is described as surfacing new brands and trends to a captive audience ([Why Sephora released a beauty set exclusively on TikTok Shop](https://digiday.com/marketing/why-sephora-released-a-beauty-set-exclusively-on-tiktok-shop/)).
- **[General beauty retail]** Only 9% of consumers find TikTok creators very trustworthy compared with 30% for Google results and 54% for word of mouth, which explains why low-trust discovery converts into search demand for verification ([Discovered on Social, Decided Everywhere](https://www.stellarising.com/blog/beauty-social-search-discovered-on-social)).
- **[General beauty retail]** Commerce-enabled content and creator shop links are helping turn TikTok discovery into direct purchase, while TikTok is cited as the first place users hear about new products ([TikTok made me buy it: Who’s discovering new products on social media in 2026?](https://yougov.com/en-us/articles/54186-tiktok-made-me-buy-it-whos-discovering-new-products-on-social-media-in-2026)).

## 4. How this persona's traits show up at Sephora

- **[Inference]** With influencer_trust 0.60 and research_depth 0.70, the shopper seeks creator and peer demonstration first, then corroborates in Gallery See it in Real Life posts and Q&A before deciding ([Beauty Insider Community FAQs](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/community-faq?icid2=customer_service_beauty_insider_community_faqs_action_link)).
- **[Inference]** With preference for demonstrated result and tutorial proof, the shopper leans on Virtual Artist try-on plus review photos and videos showing product in action, swatches and artistry to judge visible result ([Sephora Virtual Artist: Try On Makeup Instantly | Sephora Singapore](https://admin.sephora.sg/pages/virtual-artist-sg)).
- **[Inference]** With price_sensitivity 0.65, the shopper weighs price against effectiveness signals and favours Beauty Insider Cash, Rewards Bazaar samples and promotional value when efficacy is unproven ([About Beauty Insider Loyalty Program | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/loyalty-program)).
- **[Inference]** With need for shade and ingredient clarity, the shopper defers purchase when Highlights, Highlighted Ingredients and usage details cannot be verified in listings, using the inability to verify as a rejection trigger ([Glow Recipe X Beautyblender Watermelon Glow Bestsellers - Glow Recipe | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/product/glow-recipe-x-beautyblender-watermelon-glow-bestsellers-P521353)).
- **[Inference]** With convenience_priority 0.40 but desire for fast gratification on trending items, the shopper uses Buy Online Pick Up In Store or Fast Delivery to close after validation rather than waiting for standard shipping ([In-Store Pick Up & Delivery Options | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/pick-up-delivery-options?icid2=ease_and_convenience_landing_page_link_internal)).

## 6. Sephora versus Ulta Beauty for this persona
- **[Inference]** Both provide the persona's required validation loop of social inspiration plus searchable reviews and virtual try-on, but Sephora centralizes peer proof in Community Gallery and See it in Real Life while Ulta centralizes shade and skin matching in GLAMLab plus Skin Analysis ([Beauty Insider Community FAQs](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/community-faq?icid2=customer_service_beauty_insider_community_faqs_action_link)).
- **[Inference]** Sephora's Trending Products framing around social media by storm maps more directly to newness and social visibility triggers, while Ulta's filter by bestsellers and new arrivals plus exclusive brands supports broader comparison across mass and prestige alternatives ([Trending Products | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/buy/trending-products)).
- **[Inference]** Sephora's Verified Purchase badge and photo-video review guidance better satisfies the persona's rule to prioritize substantial and recent review volume with visible proof, whereas Ulta's barcode-plus-reviews flow better supports in-store to online verification ([Customer Service - Ratings & Reviews | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/ca/en/beauty/ratings-reviews?icid2=customer_service_ratings_reviews-write-review)).
- **[Inference]** For price-versus-effectiveness tradeoffs, Sephora offers sample-based Rewards Bazaar exploration for unproven trends, while Ulta offers direct dollars-off redemption starting at low thresholds, which may feel more tangible for medium price sensitivity ([About Beauty Insider Loyalty Program | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/loyalty-program)).
- **[Inference]** For speed after validation, both offer store pickup and same-day options, but Ulta explicitly promises Pickup ready in 2 hours or less and Same Day Delivery via DoorDash where eligible, matching the persona's preferred fulfilment touchpoint after research ([Same Day Delivery](https://www.ulta.com/guestservices/ways-to-shop/same-day-delivery?srsltid=AfmBOorXnU33horAg-TmuP-QpWY_lXx-YHvThENjIElC6qOeRCs546Kg)).
- **[Retailer-specific]** Neither retailer currently lets the persona earn its core loyalty points on Sephora TikTok Shop purchases, and Sephora TikTok Shop orders do not count toward VIB or Rouge status, reinforcing use of TikTok for discovery with purchase and points accrual elsewhere ([About Beauty Insider Loyalty Program | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/loyalty-program)).

## 7. Evidence quality and gaps
- **[Retailer-specific]** Loyalty mechanics, Trending pages, review submission rules, Community structure, Virtual Artist and GLAMLab capabilities, and pickup and delivery options are documented on retailer or close partner pages cited above ([About Beauty Insider Loyalty Program | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/loyalty-program)).
- **[General beauty retail]** The social-discovery to Google-verification to marketplace-purchase journey is supported by a 2026 survey of 511 beauty consumers and analysis of 22,700 Instagram posts, but the sample and Instagram-heavy method may not fully represent all US Sephora and Ulta shoppers ([Discovered on Social, Decided Everywhere](https://www.stellarising.com/blog/beauty-social-search-discovered-on-social)).
- **[Inference]** No verified evidence was found in search results for exact on-site review filter options such as filtering by rating, skin type or recency at Sephora or Ulta, so those filters are not listed as existing features despite their relevance to recent review volume checks.
- **[Inference]** No verified evidence was found for retailer-specific conversion rates for trending versus non-trending products, or for how often mixed reviews are overridden for highly desirable items, so tolerance for higher prices or mixed reviews remains an inference from persona parameters.
- **[Inference]** Prestige_priority is low at 0.30, but no retailer source was found that quantifies how this persona trades prestige against novelty on Sephora versus Ulta assortments, leaving brand-mix preference as a gap.
- **[Inference]** TikTok Shop evidence confirms both retailers are testing social checkout with limited assortments and creator-led launches, but long-term repeat purchase behaviour from those storefronts is not yet documented in the sources found ([Why Sephora released a beauty set exclusively on TikTok Shop](https://digiday.com/marketing/why-sephora-released-a-beauty-set-exclusively-on-tiktok-shop/)).
