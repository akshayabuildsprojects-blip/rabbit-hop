## 3. Retailer-specific evidence on how shoppers discover, evaluate and buy

- **[Retailer-specific]** Sephora protects prestige positioning and price integrity and competes on exclusive curation and launch access, while Ulta spans accessible to prestige price points, so identical prestige items are often price-matched but Ulta provides more drugstore/masstige alternatives in the same cart ([Sephora vs Ulta: Who's Winning the Beauty Retail War?](https://www.42signals.com/blog/sephora-vs-ulta-beauty-retail-war/)).
- **[General beauty retail]** 88% of US beauty shoppers consider ratings and reviews when making a beauty purchase, 67% consider photos/videos from other shoppers, and 89% say they trust ratings/reviews for the buy decision, based on 26,340 US consumers surveyed May 2023 ([PowerReviews survey highlights strong pockets of consumer spending](https://www.cosmeticsdesign-europe.com/Article/2023/09/19/PowerReviews-survey-highlights-strong-pockets-of-consumer-spending)).
- **[General beauty retail]** Discovery is a relay: Google is first stop for 43%, with 57% starting on Instagram, YouTube, TikTok or AI; when a product is found on TikTok, 72% search for it on Google and only 7% buy directly through TikTok at that moment, based on 511 beauty consumers ([57% of beauty consumers don’t start with Google Search](https://searchengineland.com/beauty-consumers-google-search-493828)).
- **[General beauty retail]** YouTube creators are rated very trustworthy by 14.1% versus 8.6% for TikTok creators; YouTube leads for tutorials while TikTok is nearly competitive with Google only for dupes/alternatives ([57% of beauty consumers don’t start with Google Search](https://searchengineland.com/beauty-consumers-google-search-493828)).
- **[General beauty retail]** Value scrutiny is rising: 54% of executives see restricted spending as greatest risk, McKinsey notes cooling of the \$450 billion beauty market, and shoppers are described as easily price-comparing online with AI search accelerating comparisons ([How consumer demand for value is changing beauty shopping](https://www.glossy.co/beauty/how-consumer-demand-for-value-is-changing-beauty-shopping/)).
- **[General beauty retail]** Dupe-seeking is mainstream value behavior, not only lowest-price buying: 42% seek less expensive alternatives to high-end products and 64% of Gen Z and 67% of millennials use dupes to save money, with one in three beauty shoppers having purchased makeup dupes seen on social media ([Beauty Dupes & Aspirational Beauty: How to Capture Gen Z & Millennial Spending](https://www.cosmeticsandtoiletries.com/research/consumers-market/news/22923675/beauty-dupes-aspirational-beauty-how-to-capture-gen-z-millennial-spending)).

## 6. Sephora versus Ulta Beauty for this persona

- **[Retailer-specific]** Assortment fit differs: Sephora rewards prestige launch discovery and tiered-event savings on prestige, while Ulta lets value comparison span mass to prestige plus services in one loyalty account ([Sephora vs Ulta: Who's Winning the Beauty Retail War?](https://www.42signals.com/blog/sephora-vs-ulta-beauty-retail-war/)).
- **[Retailer-specific]** Loyalty economics differ: Sephora converts points to cash at 500 points = \$10 with higher tier unlocking larger event percentage, while Ulta converts points to cash on a rising scale to \$125 at 2,000 points and allows faster earn at Platinum/Diamond ([Beauty Insider | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/beauty-insider), [Ulta Beauty Rewards: Points for Dollars Off & Perks | Ulta Beauty](https://www.ulta.com/rewards/)).
- **[Retailer-specific]** Promotion mechanics differ: Sephora centers on code-gated Beauty Offers and twice-yearly Savings Event percentages, Ulta centers on threshold coupons including prestige plus Beauty Steals-type steals with many exclusions — both require checking terms before counting savings ([Sephora Coupons, Promo Codes & Coupon Codes | Sephora](https://www.sephora.com/beauty/beauty-offers), [Ulta Coupons, Promo Code, Coupon Code | Ulta Beauty](https://www.ulta.com/coupons/)).
- **[Inference – hypothetical]** Versus Trend-Driven shopper: Trend-Driven starts from viral item and settles for a dupe; this persona starts from value and treats viral as one of several candidates — Scenario A at either retailer shows broader initial set, deeper review parity check, and willingness to buy neither if justification is insufficient.
- **[Inference – hypothetical]** Versus Sensitive-Skin and Time-Constrained shoppers: same depth as Sensitive-Skin but checks price-per-use, durability and failure complaints rather than ingredients/irritation; opposite of Time-Constrained — spends time to save money rather than spending money to save time.
- **[Inference – hypothetical]** Stop-and-decide rule applied at both: cease cross-retailer comparison when additional searching cannot plausibly change rank-order by more than expected saving net of time and return hassle, then buy best-value verified option or defer; price sensitivity never means assuming lowest ticket is best value nor that similar claims equal similar results.

## 7. Evidence quality and gaps

- **[Retailer-specific]** Strong verification for loyalty and promo existence from retailer pages themselves, but exact review-filter labels, sort options, and unit-price display on sephora.com and ulta.com were not verified in sources reviewed and are not listed as existing features.
- **[General beauty retail]** Review influence is well-quantified for US beauty (88% consider, 89% trust), but data on filtering specifically for wear, finish or durability complaints is missing; the persona's 6-25 review depth is a modeling assumption, not an observed norm ([PowerReviews survey highlights strong pockets of consumer spending](https://www.cosmeticsdesign-europe.com/Article/2023/09/19/PowerReviews-survey-highlights-strong-pockets-of-consumer-spending)).
- **[General beauty retail]** Dupe prevalence (64-67% use) and price as primary driver (58% cite price) are documented, but willingness to pay more for proven longevity is inferred from value-scrutiny commentary rather than directly measured price-performance tradeoff rates ([Beauty Dupes & Aspirational Beauty: How to Capture Gen Z & Millennial Spending](https://www.cosmeticsandtoiletries.com/research/consumers-market/news/22923675/beauty-dupes-aspirational-beauty-how-to-capture-gen-z-millennial-spending), [Gen Z Beauty Trends 2025: Authenticity, Inclusivity | Attest](https://www.askattest.com/blog/research/gen-z-beauty-trends)).
- **[Retailer-specific]** Return-path clarity matters to the switching rule, yet retailer return windows in third-party comparisons conflict (30-day full refund vs 60-day credit variations) and were not verified on retailer help pages in this pass, so no window is asserted here.
- **[Inference]** No evidence was found that every product has a direct dupe or that similar marketing claims predict similar performance; analysis assumes non-equivalence until independent performance proof is shown.
